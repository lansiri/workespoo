## Build

1. **`coveredRequirements`** exactly as section 4 of the spec says. Pure.
2. **`listApplyJobs`, `getApplyJob`, `currentRequirementKinds`.** A job counts when it has a Need Card that is not `stale`, the card's company has an `event_company` row and takes part, and at least one requirement is covered. Title and summary come from the Need Card and go through `maskEmployer` with the employer's terms when the company is masked (see how `buildCompanyView` in the needs feature does it; use `src/lib/mask`). Nothing you return may hold the employer's real name, the posting's id on the source site, or raw posting text.
3. **The questions** (prompt A). One set per Need Card id, version and language, kept in `app_meta` under `application:questions:<needCardId>:<version>:<lang>`. Insert if absent, then read what is stored, so two people starting at the same moment get the same wording. Posting sentences are masked before they go into the prompt. Every check in the prompt spec; a question that fails becomes the scripted one from `lines.ts` with `wording: "code"`. A failed or late call means scripted questions for all, and that result is not stored (the next applicant tries the model again).
4. **`lines.ts`**: the scripted lines in English, Finnish and Swedish, with these exact wordings (placeholders in braces):
   - opening: "This interview is about one job: {title}. I'll ask about {n} things the ad asks for, one at a time. I only take notes. Afterwards an AI rates your answers against the ad, and an organiser checks the order of applicants. Here is the first one." / "Tämä haastattelu koskee yhtä työtä: {title}. Kysyn {n} asiasta, joita ilmoituksessa pyydetään, yksi kerrallaan. Teen vain muistiinpanoja. Sen jälkeen tekoäly arvioi vastauksesi ilmoitusta vasten, ja järjestäjä tarkistaa hakijoiden järjestyksen. Tässä ensimmäinen." / "Den här intervjun gäller ett jobb: {title}. Jag frågar om {n} saker som annonsen efterfrågar, en i taget. Jag antecknar bara. Efteråt bedömer en AI dina svar mot annonsen, och en arrangör kontrollerar ordningen på de sökande. Här är den första."
   - bridges, used in turn: "Next." "The ad also asks for this." "Another one from the ad." / "Seuraava." "Ilmoituksessa pyydetään myös tätä." "Vielä yksi ilmoituksesta." / "Nästa." "Annonsen efterfrågar också det här." "En till från annonsen."
   - scripted question: "The ad asks for this: {requirement}. Tell me about a time you did this yourself. What was the situation, and what did you do?" / "Ilmoituksessa pyydetään tätä: {requirement}. Kerro tilanteesta, jossa teit tätä itse. Mikä tilanne oli, ja mitä teit?" / "Annonsen efterfrågar det här: {requirement}. Berätta om ett tillfälle då du gjorde det själv. Vad var situationen, och vad gjorde du?"
   - scripted follow-up: "Could you give one concrete example from your own work?" / "Voisitko kertoa yhden konkreettisen esimerkin omasta työstäsi?" / "Kan du ge ett konkret exempel från ditt eget arbete?"
   - closing: "That was the last one. Look through your answers, then send your application." / "Se oli viimeinen. Käy vastauksesi läpi ja lähetä sitten hakemuksesi." / "Det var den sista. Gå igenom dina svar och skicka sedan din ansökan."
   - closing after a stop: "We'll stop here. Look through your answers; you can send the application as it is." / "Lopetetaan tähän. Käy vastauksesi läpi; voit lähettää hakemuksen tällaisenaan." / "Vi stannar här. Gå igenom dina svar; du kan skicka ansökan som den är."
   - for an empty answer and for "delete that", reuse the general interview's lines from `@/lib/i18n/content` (`INTERVIEW_LINES`) where they fit; add your own only if they do not.
5. **`startApplication`**: the person needs an approved Signal Card (use the card feature's public function); the job must be one of `getApplyJob`; one application per person and job (the unique index backs you up: answer 409 `already_applied`). Record the `job_assessment` consent with `recordConsent`. Store the application (`newId("app")`), the plan, the state, and turn 0: the opening line followed by the first question, `kind: "opening"`, `requirementId` of the first requirement. Audit `application.started` with `subjectType: "application"`, the application id, and `{ talentId, jobId }` in the detail.
6. **`submitApplicationTurn`** as its comment and section 5 say. One answer at a time per application (a per-application lock; a second request while one runs waits). Store the answer first (trimmed, at most `TURN_MAX_STORED_CHARS`, sentences from `splitSentences`, the input mode), then `preCheck` from `@/lib/commands`: empty (scripted repair, the same question stays open; after two in a row treat it as a skip), stop, skip, delete (remove the previous answer and ask its requirement's question again). Otherwise prompt B (role `live`, purpose `application.turn`, JSON, 8 s), its checks, then: a follow-up (`kind: "follow_up"`) or the next requirement's question behind a bridge (`kind: "question"`), or the closing. Interviewer lines are yielded sentence by sentence as `turn.delta`, then `turn.done`. The same `clientTurnId` again replays what was stored. An abort (`options.signal`) stops the model call; what is already stored stays.
7. **Skip, stop, remove, submit, withdraw, `requireOwnApplication`, `loadOwnApplication`, `exportApplications`** as their comments say. `loadOwnApplication` takes points and position from `./rank` (`applicationPoints`, `ownPosition`) and kinds from `currentRequirementKinds`; in your worktree `./rank` is a placeholder that throws, so mock it in tests. `submitApplication` enqueues `application.assess` (the kind is `ASSESS_KIND` in `./jobs`) with `dedupeKey` `application.assess:<id>`. Every audit event about one application carries `talentId` in its detail.
8. **Download my data**: in `exportPersonData` (`src/features/card/mydata.ts`) add `applications: await exportApplications(db, talentId)` and include the person's application ids in the ids whose audit rows are exported. Delete and the 24-hour purge need nothing: the tables cascade from the person. Add a test line to `mydata.test.ts` that an application is gone after `deletePerson`.
9. **Routes**, thin, in the style of `src/app/api/interview/**`: the ones in section 9 of the spec under `/api/application`. `requireTalent()` for start and the jobs list; `requireOwnApplication` for everything about one application; the turn and skip routes answer with `sseResponse`.
10. **Tests** with PGlite (`createTestDb`, the needs feature's `test-helpers`) and `setLlmFake` answering by `purpose`: every numbered acceptance point of the spec that is yours (1 to 5, and 10 for withdraw); same questions for two applicants (one model call, not two); the masked employer never in a question or in `ApplyJob`; follow-up rules; replay by `clientTurnId`; the status machine (each function refused in the wrong status); removal; a failed model call at every point. Add the scripted lines to the checks the general interview's lines get (no evaluative words, no protected questions, complete in three languages).

## Watch out for

- The interviewer never comments on an answer. No "thanks", no "great". Bridges are the fixed ones above.
- The model writes for the person in prompts A and B: everything it is given must already be masked, and everything it returns is checked for the employer's terms.
- Turn indexes are 0-based over all turns; sentence numbers are 1-based. `EvidenceRef` uses the numbers as written in labels.
- Feature code may not import another feature's internals: use each feature's `index.ts`, `src/lib` and `src/contracts`. If something you need is not exported, list it under "Requests for the lead" and work around it.
