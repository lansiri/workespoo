## Build

1. **`/organiser/applicants`**: signed out, the same sign-in card the other organiser pages show. Signed in, the event's jobs with how many applicants each has, how many are still being rated, and whether the approved list is up to date ("approved 12:40", "changed since it was approved", "not approved yet"). Each links to its list.
2. **`/organiser/applicants/[jobId]`** (`RankedListView`, client, fed by `GET /api/organiser/applicants/[jobId]`): the job (company, title), a line that says how the order is made ("Ordered by points from the AI's ratings of each job interview. You can move a person, with a reason. The company sees the list only after you approve it."), the requirements with their kind, then the applicants in order. Each applicant: position, nickname, the synthetic or live-demo tag the review queue uses, points as "n of m", per requirement the level's name (from `levels.ts`) and the reason in the page's language marked as written by AI, the summary, and when pinned "Position set by you" with the reason. Actions: "Read their answers" (loads `GET /api/organiser/applicants/[jobId]/evidence?application=<id>` and shows, per requirement, the questions and the person's own sentences with the cited ones marked); "Move" (a position and a reason, both required, then `POST .../move`); "Back to the rated position" for a pinned person (`POST .../unpin`); and one "Approve this order for the company" (`POST .../approve`) with the state beside it. People whose rating has not come back are counted in one line, not listed.
3. **`/c/[token]/applicants`** (`CompanyApplicantsView`, server-loaded with `applicantsForCompany`): unknown link, the page the company's other pages show. Before any approval: "The organiser has not approved a list yet." After: per job the approved order: position, nickname, points, per requirement the level's name and reason (marked as written by AI, "not a quote"), "Position set by the organiser" when `moved`, and "Ask to meet" (`POST /api/applicants/meet`) with the same wording and states as on the picks page. After both agreed: the name, the contact and the person's own answers, under "Shown to you after you both agreed". A line that says what the levels mean (the anchors) and that nobody is left off the list by a rating.
4. **A way in from `/c/[token]`**: one link box in `CompanyDigestView` to the applicants page (`applicants-link`), three languages.
5. **Text** in `messages-organiser.ts`, English, Finnish and Swedish, in the product's plain register. Say "rated by AI", "points", "order"; never "best", "top", "weak", "strong", "score" as a verdict on a person.
6. **Styles** in `ranked.css`, imported by your components: class names starting `we-rk-`, built only from the design system's tokens. Works at 1280 px and on a phone.
7. **Test ids** as section 9 of the spec lists them. **Tests**: the organiser pages show the sign-in card without a session; the list renders positions, points, levels and reasons from a `RankedList`; move cannot be sent without a reason; the company view shows nothing before an approval, no own words and no name before `revealed`, and both after; messages complete in three languages with none of the words step 5 forbids.

## Watch out for

- The company page gets only what `CompanyApplicants` holds. Do not load anything else for it.
- The organiser sees the person's own words only on demand, through the evidence call.
- Nothing on these pages may show whether a person spoke or typed, or state the language they answered in.
- No text in component files. Test ids never depend on language.
