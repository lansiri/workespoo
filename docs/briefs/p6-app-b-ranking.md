## Build

1. **`buildAssessorInput`** (pure). Per covered requirement: its label, text, kind, the masked ad sentence, the interviewer's questions about it (turns with that `requirementId`, kinds opening, question and follow_up; for the opening only the question part), and the person's answer sentences about it, labelled `T<turn>.S<n>`. A removed answer gives nothing. A sentence in which `findProtectedCard` finds something is `[left out]` with `leftOut: true`. The input has no name, nickname, language field or input mode: write a test that serialises it and looks.
2. **Prompt C and `assessApplication`**: `chatJson` with role `analyst`, purpose `application.assess`, the schema `ApplicationAssessOutput`, temperature 0, `maxTokens` 1800, `timeoutMs` and `deadlineMs` 75 000, `db` passed so the call is logged. Requirements without answer sentences are not sent to the model at all when none has any (then no call is made and every level is 0). Stores the checked assessment, status `assessed`, `assessedAt`, and audits `application.assessed` with `subjectType: "application"` and `{ talentId, jobId, rubricVersion }` (no levels, no reasons). Idempotent: already assessed or gone means nothing happens. A failed call throws so the queue retries.
3. **`checkAssessment`** (pure): every check of section 6 and of the prompt spec, in that order, with `CODE_REASONS`, `NOT_ANSWERED_REASON` and `CODE_SUMMARY` from `levels.ts` as replacements. The summary is checked like a reason.
4. **`applicationPoints`, `orderApplicants`** (pure) exactly as section 7 says, with `RATING_WEIGHT` from the contract.
5. **The organiser's side**: `rankedListForJob`, `listJobsWithApplicants`, `moveApplicant`, `unpinApplicant`, `approveRanking`, `evidenceForOrganiser`. The `applicant_ranking` row is made on first use. Kinds come from `currentRequirementKinds` (`./requirements`; a placeholder that throws in your worktree, so inject or mock it in tests). `changedSincePublished` compares people, order and points with the approved list. Audits: `ranking.moved` `{ applicationId, talentId, from, to, reason }`, `ranking.unpinned`, `ranking.approved` `{ count }`, all with actor `organiser`, subject the job. `evidenceForOrganiser` never returns the input mode or anything that states the language.
6. **The company's side**: `applicantsForCompany` (by `companyFromToken`), built field by field: the approved list only, minus anyone whose application no longer exists, nothing at all before the first approval; `revealed` only when the introduction between this company and this person is accepted (name and contact from `talent_identity`, and the evidence). `askToMeetApplicant`: only for someone on the approved list. `ownPosition` for the person.
7. **Introductions**: `requestToMeet` in `src/features/intros/service.ts` insists on a Daily Pick. Take its core (the lock, the already-requested and cool-down checks, the insert, the audit) into an exported function that creates an introduction for a company and a person with a Need Card and no pick, have `requestToMeet` use it, and export it and a reader of the latest introduction for a pair from `src/features/intros/index.ts`. The existing behaviour and tests of `requestToMeet` must not change.
8. **Routes**: under `/api/organiser/applicants` the ones of section 9 plus `GET /api/organiser/applicants/[jobId]/evidence?application=<id>`, each starting with `requireOrganiser()`; `POST /api/applicants/meet` with `{ token, applicationId }` for the company.
9. **Tests** (PGlite, `setLlmFake` by purpose): acceptance points 6 to 10 of the spec; points and order with every tie-break; a must-have turned nice-to-have changes the draft and not the approved list; pins (several, past the end, for a deleted application, removed); approval and `changedSincePublished`; a withdrawn or deleted person disappears from the organiser's list and from the company's approved list; the company sees nothing before approval and no own words before both agreed; every organiser route refuses without the organiser session.

## Watch out for

- A rating describes evidence. Any reason the checks cannot clear is replaced, never passed through.
- The order is arithmetic. No model call anywhere in `rank.ts`.
- Nobody is removed by a rating: every assessed application is on the list.
- Build what a company sees one field at a time (see `buildCompanyPage` and `cardForCompany` for the discipline). Never spread a row into a response.
