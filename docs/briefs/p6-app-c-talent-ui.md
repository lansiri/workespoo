## Build

1. **`/apply`** (`ApplyJobsView`, client, fed by `GET /api/application/jobs`): the event's jobs. Each: the company's display name, the title, the summary, "The ad asks for" with the covered requirements (must-haves marked), the masked-demo banner when `masked`, and one action by the person's own application: none ("Apply with a job interview" to `/apply/new?job=<jobId>`), `interviewing` ("Continue"), `review` ("Look through and send"), `submitted` or `assessed` ("See your application"). Signed out or no approved card: say what is needed and link to `/interview` or `/card`. Empty state when the event has no jobs.
2. **`/apply/[id]`** (server page, then client views by state):
   - `id === "new"` with `?job=`: `ApplyStart`. The five points and the checkbox below, a language select (as on the interview's start screen), and "Start the job interview", which posts `StartApplicationRequest` and goes to `/apply/<applicationId>`. Nothing starts without the tick.
   - `interviewing`: `JobInterviewRoom`, in the room chrome (see how `src/app/(room)/interview/[id]/page.tsx` fills the top bar: progress "Question n of m" and Stop).
   - `review`: `ApplicationReview`. `submitted`: the same page saying it was sent and that the rating is on its way, polling `GET /api/application/[id]` every two seconds. `assessed`: `ApplicationResult`.
   - Not theirs or gone: the same neutral page the interview uses (`SessionEnded` pattern).
3. **The room** composes the kit as the interview room does: `InterviewLayout` with `Transcript` on the left (own answers removable), in the middle `AiDisclosure`, `InterviewerMascot`, `InterviewerLine`, `WordsBox` (live while speaking or typing; after an answer the answer with the noted sentences marked) and `InterviewControls` with `HoldToTalk` and `TypeAnswer`, and on the right "What the ad asks for": the covered requirements with their state (waiting, asked, answered, skipped), the current one marked. No level, no points and no hint of a rating anywhere in the room. "Skip question" and Stop as in the interview. Voice exactly as the interview room wires it (`useVoice`, the same consent from the server page, the interviewer's lines spoken sentence by sentence from `turn.delta`).
4. **`useJobInterview`**: owns the turns, the current line, sending and streaming, and the requirements' progress; posts a turn with a fresh `clientTurnId` and reads the event stream (`src/lib/sse-client`); a failed post can be retried with the same id; skip, stop and remove. A reducer you can test without a browser.
5. **Review and result.** Review: per requirement the question or questions and the person's own answers, "Remove this answer", and "Send my application" (needs one answer). Result: per requirement the level's name and anchor from `levels.ts`, the reason in the page's language marked as written by AI, and the person's own sentences it rests on (rendered from the turns by reference, with the kit's own-words mark); the points as "n of m points"; the line "An organiser checks the order of applicants before the company sees it"; when `position` is there, "You are number n of m on the list the company sees"; and "Withdraw my application" with a confirmation.
6. **A way in from `/matches`**: one link box in the rail of `MatchesView` to `/apply` ("Jobs at this event you can apply for"), three languages.
7. **Text**, in `messages-talent.ts` in English, Finnish and Swedish (`Messages<T>`, `useMessages`). Use these exactly for the start screen:

8. **Styles** in `apply.css`, imported by your components: class names starting `we-ap-`, built only from the design system's tokens (the custom properties in `src/components/we/we.css`). The room must fit a short window the way the interview room now does (see the block "the interview room in a short window" at the end of `we.css`: it applies to your room too, since you use the same layout classes).
9. **Test ids** as section 9 of the spec lists them. **Tests**: the reducer (every event, retry, skip, stop, removal); the server page's choice of view by status; the start screen cannot start without the tick; the result view shows levels, reasons and own words by reference; messages complete in three languages, no evaluative or protected wording in anything the interviewer side says (`findBannedEvaluative`, `findProtectedQuestion`), and no English left in the Finnish and Swedish (see how `content.test.ts` checks).

## Watch out for

- The person must never see the real employer of a masked company: show only what `ApplyJob` and `OwnApplication` give you.
- No text in component files: every string comes from the messages file. Test ids never depend on language.
- Do not copy `InterviewRoom` wholesale: no notes panel, no jobs panel, no card hand-over. Compose the kit.
- A level is shown only on the result page, never before the person has sent the application.
